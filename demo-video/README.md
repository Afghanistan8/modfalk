# ModFalk demo

[Watch or download the approved three-minute demo](ModFalk-demo-v2.mp4).

- Runtime: 3:00.
- Picture: 1920 × 1080, H.264.
- Audio: Microsoft Ryan neural male narration, 48 kHz stereo AAC.
- [Captions](ModFalk-demo-v2.vtt).
- [Narration script](narration-v2.json).

The video uses actual browser recordings of the public ModFalk deployment, with close views of evidence and queries. It demonstrates case evidence, medical review, official disaster alerts, a labelled response scenario, historical sports forecasts, persistent graph memory, and the human review queue. The case and patient records are generated examples. Operational disaster paths remain examples unless verified.

The final export passed a full-file decode check. No browser page errors were reported during capture, and the measured audio peak remained below clipping. Draft recordings and rendering intermediates are retained locally and excluded from Git.
